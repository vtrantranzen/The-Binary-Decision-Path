# The Binary Decision Path

**One question, split in two, weighed for leverage, followed down until the next step is obvious.**

*The Binary Decision Path* is a method for cutting through hard decisions one honest split at a time — and **The Binary Path: Decision Cockpit** is its companion app, a place to fly a decision before you make it. This repository is the home of the Cockpit and the project.

By **TranZen** · Net proceeds support charitable causes · [tranzenstudio.com](https://tranzenstudio.com)

---

## The method, in a nutshell

Take everything that bears on a question and divide it into just two. Ask one honest question of the two — *for the same effort, which moves the outcome more?* Follow the heavier side down, splitting again and again, until it reaches something you can actually act on. The lighter side isn't discarded; it's *parked*, to be worked later and less deeply. A co-pilot holds the process — you do the choosing.

The full method is the subject of the book. → **[Get *The Binary Decision Path*](https://tranzenstudio.com)**

## The companion app — Decision Cockpit

The Cockpit walks the method with you: find the true question, split it in two, weigh the two for leverage, follow the heavier branch down, and stop when the next step is obvious. It never decides for you — it enumerates options, arranges what you sort, draws the tree, and keeps the process honest.

- **Runs on your own device.** It's a Progressive Web App — open it in a browser, or install it. Nothing to sign up for; your session stays with you.
- **On-device by default.** Suggestions come from a built-in, offline list. Connecting a free Google Gemini key upgrades them to live suggestions; in Private mode a free key stays on-device, so your question never leaves the machine unless you allow it.
- **Keep your work.** Export a finished decision as Markdown or PDF, or replay it step by step.
